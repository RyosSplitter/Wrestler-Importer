# AJ Styles TNA OBJ to PSP: isolated owner test

This is a model experiment on `experiment/aj-styles-obj-psp`, not an application
adapter or an accepted baseline. No supplied geometry, textures or PACs are
committed. `tools/tna_obj_trial.py` and `tools/tna_obj_export.py` are separate CLIs.
The existing converter, writer, packaging and QA implementations are unchanged.

## Inputs and evidence scope

**CONFIRMED:** The supplied OBJ has SHA-256
`6bec7e19d8b06b67be9fa0c91eb9dfb3d7c2f747c14abe5d0a17658daf4f58af`.
It contains 1,813 positions, 2,341 UVs, 1,877 normals, 3,565 triangles, one material
and seven position-index-connected components. Independent position/UV/normal
indices require 2,414 rendering corners. No skeleton, weights or animation data
are present. The MTL references `AJ_STYLESD.png` with white diffuse modulation.

**CONFIRMED:** The supplied zipped PNG is 512×512 RGBA8, entirely opaque, SHA-256
`36faf0b70451d81d3407b0344ab4c96625e254a3d68d3b94145d6ddafd009459`.
It maps correctly after one explicit OBJ-bottom-origin to PSP-top-origin V flip.
Original faces are triangular, nondegenerate and agree with stored normals.

**INFERRED:** The OBJ comments identify an `ASTYLES1.WAD:AJ_STYLES` bind-pose model
from TNA iMPACT PS2. **UNKNOWN:** The originating WAD layout, original bone
hierarchy, controller semantics, source weights and original animation behavior.
The OBJ/MTL do not establish those facts. No TNA binary adapter was implemented.

## Geometry and new binding

**CONFIRMED:** The source geometry uses one uniform scale (approximately
0.0976615941), a proper `(x,-y,-z)` rotation, X centering and foot-plane alignment
to ordinary native PSP geometry. No nonuniform scale, decimation, sculpting,
triangle removal or vertex repositioning relative to that uniform alignment is
used. Attribute precision is bounded separately below. Source corners retain
independent UV/normal identities; coincident positions are not vertex identity.

**INFERRED:** Body weights are newly generated by barycentric interpolation from
nearest ordinary PSP donor triangles. Blood/effect overlays are excluded. Four
influences per vertex and eight palette slots per triangle/draw are enforced by
the existing packer. These are inferred PSP weights, not converted TNA weights.
The source cannot support direct/name/hybrid source-weight preservation.

**CONFIRMED:** The uncorrected donor transfer sends eye-controller weight to
non-eyeball skin and fails eight analytical eye-motion envelope tests.
Original position-connected components 149 and 169 each contain 31 positions
forming an eye sphere, with maximum radial residual approximately 0.001834 PSP
units at a radius of 0.169664. The supplied atlas visually identifies their iris
surfaces. Their centers differ substantially from the unchanged donor pivots.

**INFERRED:** A new facial binding fits the donor facial-controller positions to
the source eye centers using one axis-preserving uniform facial scale/translation.
This transforms controller origins and weight-transfer query coordinates, never
the source geometry. All 79 native bone names, parents, local rotation axes and
body/head-anchor local transforms remain unchanged. Twenty-four descendant facial
local positions change. Facial fit scale is approximately 1.02295746, with model-
space translation approximately `(0.030115, 0.210730, 0.606331)`.

**INFERRED:** Separate eyeballs use only their corresponding PSP eye controller;
donor eye spill outside those components is redirected to the head anchor.
The two separate mouth/teeth components (0 and 13) use skull and lower-jaw anchors,
respectively. These sample-specific anatomical roles are guarded by the exact
OBJ hash, not generalized to arbitrary OBJ component numbers.

**CONFIRMED:** The final analytical eye-envelope and native serialization replay
checks pass. **UNKNOWN:** Actual PSP eye/blink/expression clips and authentic TNA
motion equivalence. Passing synthetic probes does not establish game behavior.

## Precision and budgets

**CONFIRMED:** Full-precision inferred weights waste compressed space: the first
fitting textured control only afforded a 32×32 atlas. Newly generated weights
are therefore quantized to multiples of 1/512 using deterministic largest-
remainder allocation. Sums remain exactly one; no original source weights exist
to alter. The maximum measured displacement versus the full-precision new bind
is 0.000182929 of model height across 14 body poses and 37 controller probes,
below the explicit experimental bound of 0.00025. This threshold is a trial
criterion, not a validated PSP hardware/animation limit.

**CONFIRMED:** Existing bounded attribute precision uses 32,768 position divisions
and its existing UV/normal bounds. No triangles are flipped or collapsed. The
native float-weight vertex format is unchanged. Added zero-weight slots pad short
palettes to the observed native 3–8-slot convention; this is not proof that smaller
palettes are inherently unsupported.

**CONFIRMED:** Whole triangles are divided into four atlas pages by the existing
chart adapter. Crop samples and remapped UVs are checked at seven barycentric
locations per triangle. No triangle is clipped. Original source samples are not
upscaled; source crop context can wrap at atlas edges. Occupancy-aware palette
training excludes unused padding. Native GIM formats/dimensions, swizzling and
RGBA32 CLUT contracts use the existing observed-corpus implementation.

| Page | Output | Colors | Observed role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `aj_page_00` | 64×64 | 256 | Mostly cranial surface |
| `aj_page_01` | 64×64 | 256 | Torso/limbs and source atlas details |
| `aj_page_02` | 128×128 | 256 | Largest surface share; prominent attire |
| `aj_page_03` | 64×64 | 256 | Remaining atlas details |

**CONFIRMED:** The marginal allocator measures actual GIM/BPE and complete PAC
sizes, not pixel-area estimates alone. Final GIM bytes total 33,600. Pixel/CLUT
allocation including retained base textures is 39,104 bytes, below the donor's
observed 54,016-byte envelope. **UNKNOWN:** Universal engine texture memory
limits and total expanded geometry/runtime heap safety.

## Native metadata and validation

**CONFIRMED:** The original donor's 77 active influence spheres enclose its own
geometry. Copied unchanged, only two enclose the new AJ geometry. Existing
`bounds_probe` interprets bone-record `+64` float4 as model-space center/radius.
**INFERRED:** Their runtime role is influence/culling bounds. The trial preserves
all centers and conservatively expands 75 radii to enclose every affected source
vertex, plus 0.001 PSP unit margin. Final decoded enclosure is 77/77. No joint,
weight or geometry changes result from this metadata expansion.

**CONFIRMED:** Native bone-record numbers are little-endian. In this experiment
only approved facial `+16..+27` local-position bytes and approved `+76..+79` radius
bytes may differ from the supplied base bone table (80-byte records). All other
bone bytes must match. The separate experimental validator reconstructs and
checks that exact whitelist, verifies enclosure and preserves unrelated PAC
payloads byte-for-byte. The production validator's unchanged-skeleton requirement
remains intact; this candidate intentionally does not satisfy that production
requirement and is never sent through the app as an ordinary conversion.

**CONFIRMED:** Existing independent checks validate native allocations,
relocations, offsets, alignment, vertex/index ranges, palette membership,
finite/normalized weights, material/GIM contracts, BPE round trips and final PAC
padding. Sections 2 (model) and 9 (textures) change; section 8 stays byte-exact.
PAC order is unchanged. Preview YOBJ is extracted directly from final section 2.

## Results and remaining review

**CONFIRMED:** Final PAC: 147,456 bytes; SHA-256
`7d2e1dccf4c1ebb650548eba8272a59e7244f3032d2fc0f4da243a3c88f041e1`.
It has 64 native draw meshes, 3,222 stored vertex records, all 3,565 source triangles,
four textures and 79 PSP bones. Expanded YOBJ is 270,240 bytes. Stored section
sizes are model 116,618, retained data 7,072 and textures 22,510 bytes, plus PAC
headers/alignment. Mesh count is an outcome of compatible native palettes/draws,
not a target or an asserted PSP limit.

**CONFIRMED:** Full numerical source-shape QA runs 14 body poses and 37 head/
controller probes. Its reference is the original OBJ shape with the shared newly
inferred PSP rig. Reused report labels that mention HCTP do not imply a TNA rig
was recovered. Static p95 surface error is about 0.00000986 of model height.

**CONFIRMED:** Hand diagnostics pass independent-controller/finite/no-collapse
checks, but synthetic finger-flex has maximum triangle-area ratio about 4.90
(native donor about 1.50), and reverse extension about 3.82 (donor about 1.72).
Worst flex triangles are near the right ring/little-finger web. The newly inferred
full-precision binding already has approximately the same stretch; attribute
precision/texture conversion did not introduce it. This remains a review finding,
not a pass or a proven source-game deformation error.

**CONFIRMED:** Source-comparison QA also retains one nearby opposed head-face
case repeated across probes and low-sample regional coverage findings. Triangle
orientation preservation is independently checked; nearby inner surfaces can
trigger nearest-face diagnostics. Those flags are retained rather than suppressed.
**UNKNOWN:** Runtime appearance, grips, culling, load stability, entrances and
victory. This PAC is not QA-approved or game-tested. Previous accepted PACs and
the production app are untouched.

## Reproduction

**CONFIRMED:** Existing Python runtime, NumPy/Pillow/SciPy/Trimesh/Rtree and native
writer/QA suffice. No Blender operation or dependency installation is needed for
this non-decimated trial. Inputs are supplied privately by their owner.

```sh
local/app-runtime/bin/python -m tools.tna_obj_trial /path/AJ_STYLES.obj \
  --mtl /path/AJ_STYLES.mtl --texture /path/AJ_STYLESD.png \
  --base /path/OWN-Kurt-Angle-Ring.PAC --out local/NEW-aj-trial \
  --chart-cell 256 --weight-units 512 --position-divisions 32768

local/app-runtime/bin/python -m model_qa compare \
  --source local/NEW-aj-trial/reference.json \
  --converted local/NEW-aj-trial/AJ-Styles-TNA-PSP-EXPERIMENTAL.pac \
  --output local/NEW-aj-trial/qa --samples 6000 --resolution 128 --no-renders
# All numerical, animation and head-cull tests run. The exporter supplies
# separate textured CPU source/final comparisons.

local/app-runtime/bin/python -m tools.tna_obj_export local/NEW-aj-trial \
  --base /path/OWN-Kurt-Angle-Ring.PAC --texture /path/AJ_STYLESD.png \
  --out local/NEW-owner-bundle
```

**CONFIRMED:** Procedural tests in `tests/test_tna_obj_trial.py` cover independent
OBJ corner indices, negative/bad indices, finite/nondegenerate triangles, explicit
materials, sphere fits, bind preservation, unauthorized bone edits, conservative
bounds and deterministic new-weight quantization. No raw game assets are fixtures.
The owner bundle contains exact final YOBJ/OBJ/MTL/PNGs, matching CPU comparisons,
the complete QA report, provenance/hashes, hand review and import instructions.
The focused regression run completed 95 tests with no failures. The final QA
report retains 51 findings: 36 repeated nearby-head-face review flags and 15
insufficient-evidence region coverage findings, plus the separately reported
synthetic hand stress reviews. No finding is silently converted into a pass.
